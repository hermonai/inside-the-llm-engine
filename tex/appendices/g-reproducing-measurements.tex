\chapter{Reproducing the Book's Measurements}\label{app:reproducing}

\begin{ChapterIntent}
Every measured number in this book has a record: the hardware, software,
model file, command, workload and raw output that produced it. This appendix
explains the record format, lists the machines used, and gives the commands
to reproduce each chapter's measurements on your own hardware --- the most
useful check on a book about performance.
\end{ChapterIntent}
