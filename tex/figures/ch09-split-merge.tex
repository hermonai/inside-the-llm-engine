% Split-K decode attention (conceptual). Top: one query head's cache cut into contiguous
% chunks of 256 tokens (Flash-Decoding, Hermon); each chunk yields a partial (m, l, o) in
% parallel, and the partials are merged in index order. Bottom: llama.cpp's Metal decode
% kernel at d006858 deals 32-cell blocks round-robin to 32 workgroups of 1-4 simdgroups
% (ggml-metal.metal kernel_flash_attn_ext_vec; ggml-metal-ops.cpp), then merges the 32
% workgroups' partials with SIMD reductions (kernel_flash_attn_ext_vec_reduce).
