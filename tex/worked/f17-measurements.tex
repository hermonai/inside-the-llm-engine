\section{Worked problems}
\subsection*{1. Name the endpoints}
\textbf{Problem.} Send is at 3 ms, first token at 17 ms, last token at
40 ms and terminal at 44 ms. There are three tokens. Find TTFT and mean gap.
\textbf{Solution.} TTFT is 14 ms. Mean delivered-token gap is
$(40-17)/(3-1)=11.5$ ms. The terminal event is excluded from that mean.
\subsection*{2. Summarize without deleting}
\textbf{Problem.} Find the mean and median of $[9,10,10,11,60]$ ms.
\textbf{Solution.} The sum is 100 ms, so the mean is 20 ms.
The middle observation is 10 ms. Retain the 60 ms observation unless
a declared exclusion rule invalidates it.
\subsection*{3. A fresh process}
\textbf{Problem.} Does restarting the model process prove a cold file cache?
\textbf{Solution.} No. File pages can remain in the operating system's
cache after the process exits. State process and cache conditions separately.
\subsection*{4. Recover the useful numerator}
\textbf{Problem.} In the four-request trace, count wire, correct-completion
and accepted tokens over 40 ms under the stated 10 ms TTFT/mean-gap rule.
\textbf{Solution.} Wire: $3+2+1+2=8$; correct completion: $3+2=5$;
accepted: A's three. Rates are 200, 125 and 75 tokens/s respectively.
C's partial output and D's wrong output remain recorded.
\subsection*{5. A different p95}
\textbf{Problem.} Find nearest-rank and inclusive-linear p95 for
$[9,10,10,11,60]$ ms. Does either establish a reliable population p95?
\textbf{Solution.} Nearest rank chooses $\lceil0.95\cdot5\rceil=5$,
giving 60 ms. Inclusive coordinate is $0.95\cdot4=3.8$,
giving $11+0.8(60-11)=50.2$ ms. Five samples do not establish a
reliable extreme-tail estimate.
\subsection*{6. The unfair timer}
\textbf{Problem.} Candidate packing takes 0.02 ms and computation takes
0.80 ms; baseline total is 1.50 ms. What candidate time belongs in
the single-call total comparison? What permits a different boundary?
\textbf{Solution.} Use $0.02+0.80=0.82$ ms. These numbers are illustrative.
Excluding packing is appropriate only for a separately declared workload
where prepared columns already exist; reuse and amortization must be
accounted for. Both routes must pass the same correctness contract.
