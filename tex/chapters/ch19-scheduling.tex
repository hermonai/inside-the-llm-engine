\chapter{Scheduling Under Pressure: Preemption, Fairness and SLO-Aware Admission}\label{ch:scheduling}

\begin{ChapterIntent}
Demand eventually exceeds memory. Then the scheduler must decide which
requests run, which wait and which are evicted, and whether an evicted request
is recomputed or swapped out. This chapter covers admission control,
preemption by recomputation and by swapping, fairness between tenants,
priorities and deadlines, and admission that is aware of service-level
objectives, using a little queueing theory to explain why a system near
saturation behaves so badly. Its target metric is goodput --- throughput that
meets its latency objectives.
\end{ChapterIntent}
