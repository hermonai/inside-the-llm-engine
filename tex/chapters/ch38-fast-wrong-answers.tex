\chapter{Fast Wrong Answers: Oracles, Differential Testing and Determinism}\label{ch:fast-wrong-answers}

\begin{ChapterIntent}
Every optimization in this book is an opportunity to change the answer.
Some changes are bugs, some are rounding that should be tolerated, and some ---
results that depend on batch size or thread count --- are defects that no
single run reveals. This chapter builds the discipline that keeps speed
honest: independent oracles, differential tests across kernels, backends and
batch shapes, tolerances derived from numerics rather than chosen to pass,
deterministic reduction orders and batch-invariant kernels, and evaluation of
quantized models by their distributions rather than by anecdote.
\end{ChapterIntent}
