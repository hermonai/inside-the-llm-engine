\chapter{Modern Speculation: The EAGLE Family, Medusa and Multi-Token Prediction}\label{ch:modern-speculation}

\begin{ChapterIntent}
The best drafters now come from the target model itself. This chapter
follows the EAGLE family --- drafting from the target's hidden features,
dynamic draft trees, and EAGLE-3's direct token prediction with fused
multi-layer features --- alongside Medusa's extra heads, multi-token-prediction
modules trained with the model, and parallel drafting that produces a whole
draft in one pass. It explains tree attention for verifying many candidate
continuations at once and compares the methods by what they cost to train, to
run and to integrate.
\end{ChapterIntent}
