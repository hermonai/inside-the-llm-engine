\chapter{Continuous Batching}\label{ch:continuous-batching}

\begin{ChapterIntent}
Batching is the lever that moves decode up the roofline, but batching
whole requests makes every short request wait for the longest one. Continuous
batching schedules at the granularity of a single model step: finished
sequences leave and new ones join between iterations. This chapter derives
iteration-level scheduling, explains which operations can share a batch and
which cannot, measures the throughput--latency curve it produces, and looks at
how production schedulers overlap CPU scheduling work with GPU
execution.
\end{ChapterIntent}
