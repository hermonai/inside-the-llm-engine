\chapter{Expert Parallelism at Scale}\label{ch:expert-parallelism}

\begin{ChapterIntent}
Placing experts on different GPUs turns every mixture-of-experts layer
into an all-to-all exchange: tokens are dispatched to their experts' devices
and the results are combined back. This chapter prices that exchange, covers
the dispatch and combine kernels built for it, load imbalance and redundant
expert replicas, and overlapping communication with computation, and explains
when wide expert parallelism across many GPUs beats keeping experts
local.
\end{ChapterIntent}
