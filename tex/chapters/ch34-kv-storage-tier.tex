\chapter{The KV Cache as a Distributed Storage Tier}\label{ch:kv-storage-tier}

\begin{ChapterIntent}
Once a KV cache outlives its request --- through prefix caching,
multi-turn sessions or disaggregation --- it becomes data to store, index,
evict and move across a hierarchy of HBM, host memory, SSD and remote storage.
This chapter derives the central break-even, loading cached KV versus
recomputing it, for each tier, and covers placement, indexing, cache-aware
routing and invalidation in systems that treat the KV cache as a distributed
storage tier.
\end{ChapterIntent}
