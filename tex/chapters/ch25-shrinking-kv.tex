\chapter{Shrinking the KV Cache: MQA, GQA and Multi-Head Latent Attention}\label{ch:shrinking-kv}

\begin{ChapterIntent}
The KV cache's size is an architectural choice. Sharing keys and values
across query heads (multi-query and grouped-query attention) cuts it by the
sharing factor; compressing them into a low-rank latent (multi-head latent
attention) cuts it further, at the price of extra computation that careful
kernels absorb into the projections. This chapter derives each design,
computes per-token cache sizes for real models, explains MLA's absorbed
projections and decoupled rotary dimensions, and shows what each choice asks
of the attention kernel.
\end{ChapterIntent}
