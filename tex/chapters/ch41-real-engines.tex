\begin{ChapterIntent}
Four real engines, read at pinned commits through the ledger: vLLM,
SGLang, llama.cpp and Hermon. For each, the chapter follows one request from
arrival to its last token, identifies where the engine spends its complexity
and what runs by default rather than behind a flag, and connects its design to
its target hardware and users. The comparison uses each engine's own source and
documentation, not its marketing.
\end{ChapterIntent}
