\chapter{Operating Inference in Production}\label{ch:production}

\begin{ChapterIntent}
An engine that is fast on one GPU is not yet a service. This chapter
covers what changes for a fleet: model loading and cold starts, autoscaling
on signals that track user-visible latency, routing that is aware of load,
cached prefixes and adapters, isolation between tenants, observability,
handling of timeouts and partial output, safe rollouts of new models, and the
cost controls that decide whether a deployment makes money.
\end{ChapterIntent}
