\chapter{The Roofline: Why Decode Leaves Your GPU Idle}\label{ch:roofline}

\begin{ChapterIntent}
A consumer GPU generating text with a 7-billion-parameter model moves
nearly as many bytes per second as its memory can deliver while its
arithmetic units sit mostly idle. This chapter explains why with the roofline
model: arithmetic intensity, the two ceilings of memory bandwidth and peak
compute, and the ridge point where they meet. Decode, at about one FLOP per
byte, sits far down the bandwidth slope; prefill, at hundreds, sits under the
compute roof. Batch size is the lever that moves decode along the slope, and
the chapter measures it. It ends with what the roofline leaves out ---
latency, launch overhead and occupancy --- because the best kernels in later
chapters live in that gap.
\end{ChapterIntent}
