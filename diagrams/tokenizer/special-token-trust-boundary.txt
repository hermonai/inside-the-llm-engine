SPECIAL-TOKEN TRUST BOUNDARY — when may text become control identity?

 untrusted user text ══▶ ordinary tokenizer ══▶ ordinary token IDs
          │
          └──✕ must not forge BOS / EOS / role / tool-control IDs

 trusted template renderer
          │ validated model-tokenizer-template binding
          ▼
 control identities ══▶ typed special TokenIds ══▶ model input sequence

Surface strings and special identities are distinct even when their text looks identical.
