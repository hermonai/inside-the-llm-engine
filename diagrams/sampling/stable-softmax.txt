STABLE SOFTMAX — how do logits become finite probabilities?

 logits z [V_vocab]
        │
        ▼
 maximum m = max(z) ──▶ shifted logits zᵢ − m ──▶ exp(zᵢ − m)
                                                    │
                                                    ▼
                                      sum S = Σⱼ exp(zⱼ − m)
                                                    │
                                                    ▼
                                      probabilities pᵢ = exp(zᵢ − m) / S
