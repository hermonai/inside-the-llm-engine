CONTROL PLANE AND DATA PLANE — who decides, and what bytes move?

┌──────────────────────────── CONTROL PLANE ─────────────────────────────┐
│ validate ──▶ resolve ──▶ admit ──▶ schedule ──▶ stop/fail ──▶ account │
└────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┘
                                 │ owns policy and lifecycle
                                 ▼
┌────────────────────────────── DATA PLANE ──────────────────────────────┐
│ (artifact) ══▶ (weights) ══▶ model step ══▶ logits ──▶ token ══▶ bytes │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

The control plane may schedule data movement; it does not become the tensor payload.
