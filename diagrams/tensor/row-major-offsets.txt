ROW-MAJOR OFFSET DERIVATION

 shape d = [2, 3, 4]

                               right-to-left product
                                      │
               ┌──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┐
               ▼                      ▼                      ▼
 axis          0                      1                      2
 dimension     2                      3                      4
 stride       12                      4                      1

 logical index i = [1, 2, 3]

              offset(i) = 1 × 12 + 2 × 4 + 3 × 1
                        =   12   +   8   +   3
                        = 23 elements

For `f32`, byte displacement = 23 elements × 4 bytes/element = 92 bytes.
