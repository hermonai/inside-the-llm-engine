CACHE REUSE THROUGH TILING

 A tile [Bₘ,Bₖ] ──┐
                  ├──▶ tile products ──┐
 B tile [Bₖ,Bₙ] ──┘                     │
                                        ▼
 previous C tile [Bₘ,Bₙ] ───────────▶ accumulate ──▶ updated C tile

A and B are read-only independent operands. C alone accumulates.
Outer loops ii,kk,jj select regions; inner i,k,j visits contributions.
Reusing A across j and B across i seeks to avoid repeated slow-memory loads.
Actual cache residency depends on hardware, competing data and tile extents.

Tail tiles shrink at the M, K, or N edge; no dimension must divide evenly.
Example: [5,7] × [7,3], blocks [4,4,2]; last i=4..5,k=4..7,j=2..3.
