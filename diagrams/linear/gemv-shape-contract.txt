GEMV SHAPE CONTRACT

 A [M,K] ──┐
            ├──▶ GEMV: one row dot x per i ──▶ y [M]
 x [K] ────┘

Require A.shape[1] = x.shape[0]. Reduce K; preserve M.
Both operands are independent, immutable views; y is a fresh owner.
For each output i: yᵢ = Σₖ Aᵢₖ xₖ, increasing k from 0 through K−1.
Empty K yields zeros; empty M yields an empty output.
