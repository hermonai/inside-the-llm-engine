WHAT DOES EPSILON DO FOR THE ZERO VECTOR?

                         x = [0,0,…,0]
                                │
                                ▼
                         mean(x²) = 0
                   ┌────────────┴────────────┐
                   ▼                         ▼
             ε = 0: REJECTED            ε > 0, finite
             1 / √0 undefined               │
                   │                         ▼
                   └──✕ typed error      r = 1 / √ε finite
                                             │ multiply by xᵢ=0
                                             ▼
                                        y = [0,0,…,0]

Epsilon stabilizes the denominator; it does not turn a zero activation nonzero.
