HOW DOES ONE VECTOR PRODUCE ITS RMS SCALE?

[x₀, x₁, …, xD₋₁]
          │ square each element
          ▼
[x₀², x₁², …, xD₋₁²]
          │ reduce sum
          ▼
       Σ xᵢ²
          │ divide by D
          ▼
  mean square m₂
          │ add ε inside root
          ▼
    √(m₂ + ε) ──▶ reciprocal ──▶ r = 1 / √(m₂ + ε)

Squaring prevents sign cancellation; the square root restores the input's units.
