WHY IS THE REFERENCE RMSNORM A TWO-PASS OPERATOR?

PASS 1 — REDUCE                           PASS 2 — SCALE

(input x [D])                             (input x [D])
      │ read D                                  │ read D again
      ▼                                         ▼
Σ xᵢ² ──▶ mean ──▶ add ε ──▶ rsqrt ──▶ scalar r
                                                  │ broadcast
(scale w [D]) ═════════════════════════════════════╪══▶ xᵢ · r · wᵢ
                                                  │          │
                                                  │          ▼
                                                  └────▶ [output y [D]]

Output cannot be finalized until every input has contributed to scalar r.
