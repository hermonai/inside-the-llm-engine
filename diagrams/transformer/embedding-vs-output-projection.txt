WHY ARE EMBEDDING AND OUTPUT PROJECTION DIFFERENT?

                         MODEL SPACE WIDTH D

TokenId t ──▶ row lookup ──▶ [hidden x [D]] ──▶ GEMV + bias ──▶ [logits [V]]
                ▲                                  ▲
                │                                  │
        (embedding E [V,D])              (output W [V,D], b [V])

Embedding: one ID selects one row; little arithmetic.
Projection: every output row forms a dot product with x.

Some architectures tie E and W storage; Chapter 7 and ENGINE-1 do not assume that.
