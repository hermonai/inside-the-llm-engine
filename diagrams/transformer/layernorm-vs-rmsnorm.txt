WHICH STATISTIC DOES EACH NORMALIZATION USE?

             LayerNorm                              RMSNorm
                 │                                     │
                 ▼                                     ▼
          compute mean μ                         compute mean x²
                 │                                     │
                 ▼                                     ▼
          center xᵢ − μ                          no recentering
                 │                                     │
                 ▼                                     ▼
       variance-like scaling                   root-mean-square scaling
                 │                                     │
                 └──────── learned gain ───────────────┘

They are different operators; RMSNorm does not silently subtract the activation mean.
