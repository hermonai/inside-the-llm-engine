## Limits

Single-machine results on a desktop in use. The Chapter 1 lab's weights are
random; it measures the cost of streaming and multiplying them, not a model.
The Chapter 2 counts are exact properties of the files; its FLOPs and bytes
per token are derived from them, not timed, and its expert-union figures
assume uniform routing.
